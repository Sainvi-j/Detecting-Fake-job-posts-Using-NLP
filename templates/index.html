Tips to Avoid Fake Jobs

  • Never pay money to apply or "secure" a job
  • Be cautious of "work from home" with high pay + no experience
  • Check if the company has a real website and LinkedIn
  • Avoid jobs asking for "training fees" or "registration"
  • Real jobs rarely use words like "urgent", "limited seats"
